ROB Trend

Each line = one selected vessel. Y axis = Remaining Onboard (ROB) in metric tonnes (MT) at each report time. Switch to Consumption mode (top-right) to see daily burn as a line. When vessels have very different ROB magnitudes (a big-value vessel flattens the others), click Scale: Log (top-right) — it compresses the Y axis so every curve stays visible. 加油 (bunkering) days show as a green ▲ at the top (never negative) and are excluded from the Avg/day in Vessel Summary. Bunkering is currently inferred from ROB increases; to record explicit bunkering events, add them to rob_data/bunkering.json ({"船名": {"YYYY-MM-DD": 加油量MT}}). Use Oil to pick the product and Daily anchor to align every vessel to the same time-of-day so daily differences are comparable.
