Gulf Conflict Cuts Far East–ISC/ME/RS Capacity by 14% YoY; CULines Ramps +240%
海湾冲突致远东-印巴/中东/红海运力同比降14%；CULines增投240%
Alphaliner's late-July review shows deployed capacity on the Far East–Indian Subcontinent/Middle East/Red Sea trade fell by around 312,000 teu, or more than 14% year-on-year, to just under 1.9 Mteu. Most of the reduction traces to the widening Gulf conflict and security concerns around the Strait of Hormuz. Smaller players (capacity share <1%) accounted for ~90% of the decline, led by HMM (-115,000 teu) and SeaLead (-66,000 teu). By contrast, CULines recorded the largest percentage increase, up around 240% (+30,000 teu) after adding four services on top of its existing REX loop.
Alphaliner 7月底评估显示，远东-印巴/中东/红海航线部署运力同比下降约31.2万TEU（降幅超14%），至不足190万TEU。运力收缩主要源于海湾冲突扩大及霍尔木兹海峡周边安全担忧。运力份额低于1%的中小船公司贡献约90%的降幅，其中HMM减少11.5万TEU、SeaLead减少6.6万TEU。相反，CULines录得以百分比计的最大增幅，约240%（+3万TEU），在REX航线基础上新增4条服务。

SeaLead Shipping Files for Voluntary Liquidation After US Sanctions
SeaLead航运在美国制裁后申请自愿清算
Singapore-based SeaLead Shipping has filed for voluntary liquidation, ending nearly a decade of operations. OFAC first sanctioned 16 chartered vessels in July 2025, then targeted the company and subsidiaries in India, the Marshall Islands and the UAE. SeaLead had ranked 13th globally in 2024 with 208,030 teu, but its fleet halved and it fell to 21st place by year-end. Liquidator Cosimo Borrelli of Admiralty Asia Partners was appointed on 3 August.
新加坡SeaLead航运已申请自愿清算，结束近十年运营。美国OFAC于2025年7月首次制裁其租赁的16艘船舶，随后针对该公司及其在印、马绍尔群岛和阿联酋的子公司。SeaLead 2024年全球排名第13（20.8万TEU），但船队减半，年底跌至第21位。清算人Admiralty Asia Partners的Cosimo Borrelli于8月3日获委任。
