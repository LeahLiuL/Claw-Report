Typhoon Dolphin Devastates Chinese Ports — 2.46M TEU Stranded, Recovery Takes Weeks
台风"海豚"重创中国港口 — 246万TEU运力滞留，恢复需数周
Typhoon Dolphin, the third and strongest typhoon to hit China in five weeks, forced Shanghai and Ningbo ports to suspend operations Aug 7–9. 177 vessels were stranded (117 at Shanghai, 60 at Ningbo), tying up 2.46M TEU — 3.66% of the global container fleet. Shanghai's average vessel wait reached 87 hours (3.95 days); arrival delays hit 5.63 days. Congestion has spread to Qingdao and Shenzhen. Carriers are omitting Shanghai calls and rolling cargo. Recovery expected to take several weeks.
台风"海豚"是五周内袭击中国的第三场、也是最强的一次台风，迫使上海港和宁波港于8月7-9日暂停作业。177艘船舶滞留（上海117艘、宁波60艘），246万TEU运力被占用——占全球集装箱船队的3.66%。上海港平均等泊时间达87小时（3.95天），到港延误约5.63天。拥堵已蔓延至青岛和深圳。多家船公司取消上海挂靠并甩柜，恢复预计需数周。
