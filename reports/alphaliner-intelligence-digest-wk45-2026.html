Two takeovers reshape Europe's short-sea map (issue 44). Eurogate, acting through subsidiary Sogemar (Contship Italia/Eurokai joint structure), signed an in-principle deal on Oct 28 to acquire La Spezia-based Tarros Group — an 1828-founded Mediterranean carrier-logistics operator running five chartered feeders (700-1,800 teu) from Portugal to Turkey and the Levant — for a price 'in the mid-double-digit million range', completion expected year-end pending regulatory approval. Neither giant container line is involved: regional feeders are consolidating among themselves.
